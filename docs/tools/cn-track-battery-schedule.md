# China Lithium Battery Production Schedule Tracker

The [local dashboard](http://localhost:8765/cn-track-battery-schedule/) displays mainland China and CATL in two full-width charts. Each chart has independent date controls and shows its sourced-month count. The calendar starts in January 2025. Each recorded value links to its dated source. [中文采集流程](../i18n/cn-track-battery-schedule.zh-CN.md)

## Sources and scope

- **Mainland China** uses China-market surveys from TD Research and the Guangdong Battery Industry Association of traction, stationary storage and consumer batteries combined. The supplier sample changes between releases. The survey does not provide a plant-by-plant geographic reconciliation. For older months the collector also reads reports that reproduce the survey. [January 2025 survey](https://caifuhao.eastmoney.com/news/20250114144646919311280) · [June–August compilation](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2025-10-22/doc-infuuprm0306767.shtml) · [November 2026 release](https://cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/7504351895/1bf4b529700102rrpq)
- **CATL** combines named reports with estimates attributed to the company by market share. For each month the chart prioritizes a named estimate, then the monthly survey’s Battery A, then Citi’s Company A. The table shows all available attributions side by side within the month. The sources vary in reliability and geographic coverage. The February–April 2026 figures come from a public investor note whose underlying survey is unpublished. The July–September figures are from ZE Consulting's August survey as reported by Citi. These series have different denominators and cannot support a mainland market-share calculation. [January 2025 research note](https://m-robo.datayes.com/feed/detail?id=446338) · [February–April note](https://xueqiu.com/9265667347/380971967) · [Citi report, page 1](https://public.fxbaogao.com/report-image/2026/08/16/5611263-1.png) · [November revision](https://www.qcgys.com/show_article-97125.html)

The 2025 TD monthly releases identify the largest reported producer as “Battery A”. Citi’s July 27, 2026 table also labels its rows anonymously. The company codes are specific to each publication. The July table identifies the July and August volumes by chemistry for Company A, but provides no public company key. The source figures are presented under the working assumption that both A labels refer to CATL. Differences across providers or forecast dates can affect comparisons. [TD release](https://caifuhao.eastmoney.com/news/20251104135104420638020) · [Citi/ZE original table, figure 1](https://n.sinaimg.cn/spider20260729/636/w1051h385/20260729/3217-3dd8f21284d3a2f1873d3c5a2d5333e7.png)

## Update

1. Check the [repository data update SOP](../development.md#data-update-sop) for Actions coverage. This tool currently runs locally.
2. Search TD's monthly China survey by production month. Start with the author's releases and check the following month's release for revisions. If the original is unavailable, match the survey month, supply scope and total in an archived report. For April and May 2025 the reproduced figures can also be checked against Hicend Futures' reports. [April cross-check](https://www.hicend.com.cn/ftpfile/static/file/upload/file/202504/16/0eMmWnbPxQ16u0Dm.pdf) · [May cross-check](https://www.hicend.com/ftpfile/static/file/upload/file/202505/12/WOkOo8fqVhJJEfer.pdf)
3. For CATL, look for company disclosures, then named research and attributed news reports. Record the production month and GWh together. Verify the report date separately. Check the surrounding paragraph for the company, scope, comparison month and whether the value is a plan, a revision or a lower bound. For inferred records, require the scheduled GWh for the identified survey’s A row and the specific month. Sector totals, installations, shipments, growth rates and quarterly targets are excluded.
4. Add the dated source URL to the corresponding list in `investdiy/cn_track_battery_schedule/sources.py`. Add an extraction rule if the report introduces a new format. One report may cover several months. Preserve the original plan if the report explicitly quantifies its revision. A later dated observation supersedes the previous record within the same series, month and attribution.
5. Run the collector from the repository root. It also checks TD's recent author feed. A fetch or parsing failure leaves the previous CSV in place. Once the command succeeds, check the printed coverage, inspect the two series in the monthly table, and open the sources for each new or revised value. Record unresolved months as gaps. Run the extraction tests after a format change.

```bash
.venv/bin/python3 -m investdiy.cn_track_battery_schedule
.venv/bin/python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_battery_schedule.py
```

## Reading and validating the CSV

The local output is `investdiy/cn_track_battery_schedule/data/out/monthly.csv`. The unique key is `series`, `month` and `attribution`. CATL records use `named`, `survey_a` or `citi_a`. The two A codes retain their source-specific identities. The market series leaves attribution empty. The `as_of` date belongs to the linked publication, so it can precede the production month. The CSV includes the source, URL, GWh, qualifier and any earlier plan reported for that month.

The qualifier distinguishes an estimate, a revision, an approximate revision, a strict lower bound and an inclusive lower bound. For example, the November 2026 TD release revises the previous mainland estimate down to about 340 GWh, and the November 22 company report revises CATL's November schedule from 96 to 93 GWh. In the table a dash marks an unverified month. The charts leave the corresponding slot empty.

Later reports sometimes give only a direction or a monthly growth rate. The CSV keeps the dated GWh estimate and records that follow-up separately in the `followup_*` fields. The November 2026 revision also includes the original and revised LFP volumes and the unchanged ternary volume. The dashboard displays these details under the corresponding month. [September update](https://www.guandian.cn/article/20260918/603598.html) · [October update](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-10-16/doc-inivmazr8330052.shtml)

The Citi July and August totals are collected from the July 29 article. The chemistry split in Figure 1 is transcribed in the local, git-ignored `data/in/citi_20260727_figure1.csv` under the tool directory (columns `month,lfp_gwh,ncm_gwh`). The collector checks each split against the corresponding published total. A clean checkout can collect totals without this optional file.
